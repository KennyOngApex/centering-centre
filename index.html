Filming the tilt video

  1. Put one light 30–45° to the side.
  2. Tilt the card 15° each way over about 6 seconds.
  3. Keep all four corners in frame.

Hold the phone square to the card and fill the frame with it. A photo taken at an angle changes the measured centering. The white dot filter reads a thin band along each edge.

Why the grade is out of reach

PSA's 10 limits match the text quoted from PSA's grading standards page. The other limits come from third-party summaries and may differ from each grader's own page. TAG figures marked ~ are approximate. Beckett back centering is not scored because sources disagree. A phone photo measures to about one point.
